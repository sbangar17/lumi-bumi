Lumi ♥ Bumi

Giving Bumi to your niece while Lumi stays with you

How it works

Both cubes talk through the internet broker (EMQX), not directly to each other. So Bumi works from any home with Wi-Fi. Only Bumi's Wi-Fi needs changing.

The same broker also connects the phone page, so each family can send text, drawings and photos too.

1Before you hand it over at your place

Keep your passwords private. Don't put them in a gift note or in a photo of the setup. Bumi holds only its own EMQX login, never Lumi's.

2At your niece's home a grown-up, once

You'll need her home Wi-Fi name and password, and a phone.

  1. Plug Bumi into the USB charger with the USB-C cable.
  2. Unplug it, then plug it in again while holding both buttons. The screen shows the setup hotspot.
  3. On the phone, join Wi-Fi LumiBumi-Setup.
  4. Open 192.168.4.1 in the phone's browser.
  5. Pick her home Wi-Fi from the list and enter its password. Save.
  6. Bumi restarts and joins. It plays the online chime and shows the idle screen.
  7. From your Lumi (or the phone page), send "Hi!". Bumi should chime and show it.

Her Wi-Fi must be

  • 2.4 GHz. Most routers broadcast it. If the router has one name for both bands, that's fine.
  • A normal password network. Hotel or school Wi-Fi that asks you to sign in on a web page won't work.
